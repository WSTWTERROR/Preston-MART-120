The Story of Jared

They say The Mighty Jared is somebody to be feared, but nobody truly knows who he is. A young lad from a small village in northern Fidith turned warrior, hero, and lion of Fidith. He serves directly under the current king: Everard Percival. He is rarely seen away from his mentor, but they say he is much friendlier alone than he is with the king. That does not mean you should mess with him. He is not a man to be underestimated. Though I cannot control what you do, know this: The Might Jared is not a a nickname unearned.
